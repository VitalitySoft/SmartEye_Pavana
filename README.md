## 📋 Original Specification & Content Reference

### SmartEye eQMS
**Best eQMS for Medical Devices & SaMD Compliance**  
SmartEye is a cloud-based electronic Quality Management System (eQMS) designed for medical device companies and Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) organizations. It brings quality, regulatory, design-control, risk-management, testing, documentation, and traceability activities into one platform.  
Source: [https://eqms-smarteye.com/](https://eqms-smarteye.com/)

#### Overview
SmartEye is developed by S-Cube Technologies Limited to help medical device and healthcare organizations move from manual and paper-based quality processes to a centralized digital platform.

The platform is positioned around four main outcomes:
1. Faster quality processes
2. Reduced quality administration
3. Faster product release
4. Faster external audits

#### Key Capabilities
- **Requirements Management:** Create and manage different levels of requirements and connect them to parent requirements, validation methods, hazards, and verification tests.
- **Test Management:** Manage verification and validation activities and connect tests with requirements. Test execution information can be represented through reporting matrices.
- **Risk Management:** Integrates medical-device risk-management workflows, including support for ISO 14971. Safety-related hazards can be linked with requirements and other lifecycle artifacts.
- **End-to-End Traceability:** Dynamic traceability between user needs, requirements, risks, tests, and other development artifacts.
  - Dynamic Trace Matrix
  - Risk Management Matrix
  - Test Execution Report Matrix
- **Design Control:** Design inputs/outputs, design reviews, verification and validation, requirements management, risk management, documentation and approvals.
- **SaMD Lifecycle Management:** Supports software lifecycle frameworks for SaMD and can accommodate Agile development approaches. Connects software requirements, user needs, validation methods, verification tests, hazards, and risk controls.
- **Document Management:** Review and approval workflows, version control, electronic signatures, controlled quality records, audit-ready document generation, automatic generation of DHF and DMR files.

#### Regulatory and Standards Support
- ISO 13485:2016
- ISO 14971

#### QMS Processes
- **Management:** Management responsibility, Quality planning, Management review, Resource management
- **Product Realization and Lifecycle Control:** Design & development control, Risk management, Purchasing & supplier management, Production & process control, Change management
- **Compliance and Documentation:** Document control, Record control, Electronic records & signatures
- **Monitoring and Improvement:** Internal audits, Nonconformance management, CAPA, Feedback analysis, Post-market surveillance
- **Support and Compliance Enablement:** Training & competence, Calibration & equipment control, Software validation, Labeling & UDI compliance
